# Audience

Welcome to the **Audience** repository — this space is dedicated to the external community that follows and interacts with us from the outside.

## Who Are the Audience?

The **Audience** includes individuals who:
- Follow our updates
- Attend public events or sessions
- Engage with our content
- Are interested in our work but are not formally part of the internal community

They are an essential part of our ecosystem, often becoming Guests or Members over time.

## Introduction: Understanding Knowledge Access Across Circles

In our community, we follow a model called **Elmentor Circles**, which represents different levels of involvement and access. These circles are structured like layers, moving from general public to core leadership:

1. **Audience** – External audience who engage with us from the outside  
2. **Guests** – Occasional participants with limited access  
3. **Members** – Active, permanent participants in the community  
4. **Group Advisory (GAs)** – Leaders who support and guide members  
5. **Lead Group Advisory (LGAs)** – Guide both the Group Advisory and members  
6. **Executives** – Set strategic direction and oversee all layers  

This model helps us structure engagement, learning, and decision-making in a clear and scalable way.
